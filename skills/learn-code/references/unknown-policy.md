# Unknown policy v1.1

- `blocking`: without resolving it, the active question cannot be answered or the current flow may be materially wrong.
- `non_blocking`: its current role is enough for this slice; preserve it for later.
- `deferred`: valuable but belongs to another learning target or deeper level.

Every unknown records why it matters, current assumption, confidence, resolution plan, and status. Do not disguise an unknown as a fact. Do not expand a non-blocking unknown merely because its type or implementation is unfamiliar.
